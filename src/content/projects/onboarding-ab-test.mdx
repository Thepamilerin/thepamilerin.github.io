problem: "The hypothesis was that earlier friction filters out uncommitted users and raises long-term retention among those who stay. The decision rule was to ship only if day-7 retention improves and total engagement holds."

approach: "Powered the test for a one percentage point effect before looking at results, checked the split for sample ratio mismatch, then ran a two-proportion z-test with Wilson intervals alongside a Beta-Binomial Bayesian model. Broke results down by casual, core and power users with a Bonferroni correction."

      - "Go straight to the retention comparison"
  - decision: "Fix the decision rule before the analysis"
    reasoning: "Ship only if day-7 retention improves and the guardrail holds. A rule set in advance cannot be bent by what the data shows."
