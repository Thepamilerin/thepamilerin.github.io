---
title: "Score with a rubric, not a classifier"
date: 2025-03-01
context: "ZTM needed to match candidates to roles. There was no large labelled outcome dataset, and past hiring decisions carry the bias of the people who made them."
decision: "Build a five-dimension weighted scoring engine and design the fairness controls into it, instead of training a classifier."
alternatives:
  - option: "A classifier trained on historical hiring outcomes"
    pros:
      - "Learns patterns automatically"
    cons:
      - "Would replicate historical hiring bias"
      - "Needs labelled outcomes that did not exist at scale"
reasoning: "A model I can explain, audit and correct is worth more here than one that scores slightly better on a metric nobody can verify. Fairness and an audit trail were requirements, not extras."
tags:
  - "fairness"
  - "architecture"
relatedProjects:
  - "ztm-matching-engine"
