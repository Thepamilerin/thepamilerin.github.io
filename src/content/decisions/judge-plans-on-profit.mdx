context: "The three stakeholders each argued for a plan in terms of revenue or return per pound."
decision: "Score each plan on profit at a 60% gross margin, with a break-even return of £1.67 per £1."
alternatives:
  - option: "Rank by revenue"
    pros:
      - "Easy to compare"
    cons:
      - "Rewards spending that does not pay back"
  - option: "Rank by average return"
    pros:
      - "Familiar"
    cons:
      - "Ignores that the next pound returns less than the average"
reasoning: "A 15% cut loses £1.17m of revenue and still gains about £0.29m of profit, because the £0.975m saved is more than the margin lost. Revenue alone would have rejected it."
