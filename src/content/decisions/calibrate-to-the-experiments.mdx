title: "Calibrate the model to the two experiments"
date: 2026-10-28
context: "Sales data alone could not separate TV from seasonality and growth, and the platform reports disagreed with each other. A TV geo trial and a Meta lift study existed."
decision: "Use both experiments as priors and test the finished model against each of them independently."
alternatives:
  - option: "Trust the sales data alone"
    pros:
      - "Simplest to explain"
    cons:
      - "TV looked like it returned £0.92 once the VAT error was left in"
  - option: "Use platform-reported attribution"
    pros:
      - "Already available"
    cons:
      - "The platforms credit themselves and disagree with each other"
reasoning: "The experiments are the only direct evidence of cause in the data. A model that agrees with them is worth more than one that fits the sales history slightly better."
